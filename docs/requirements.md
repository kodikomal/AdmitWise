# AdmitWise — Requirements Specification

## 1. Project Title
**AdmitWise — College Admission Prediction and Recommendation System**

## 2. Abstract
Higher education admissions in India, particularly within metropolitan regions such as Mumbai and across Maharashtra, present significant complexities for students transitioning from Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) or entrance examinations into undergraduate degree programs. Students often struggle to evaluate their admission likelihood accurately across competitive degree programs (e.g., B.Sc. IT, B.Sc. CS, B.Tech, BMS, B.Com) due to multi-round merit lists, varying reservation categories, and strict mandatory subject prerequisites.

**AdmitWise** is a full-stack, rule-based web application that provides transparent, explainable admission predictions, college recommendations, multi-college comparisons, and downloadable counseling reports based on verified historical cutoff archives and state admission guidelines.

---

## 3. Problem Statement
1. **Opaque Commercial Aggregators:** Conventional education portals present misleading "probabilities" generated by opaque black-box models or commercial sponsorships.
2. **Ignored Mandatory Eligibility:** Students frequently overlook critical prerequisites—such as the mandatory requirement of Mathematics in HSC for B.Sc. Information Technology under Mumbai University circulars—leading to rejected applications.
3. **Cutoff Fluctuation Across Rounds:** Admission cutoffs drop between Round 1, Round 2, and Round 3, yet static lists fail to contextualize this variance.
4. **Data Scarcity & Unverified Claims:** Cutoff thresholds vary by reservation category (Open, OBC, SC, ST, EWS) and quotas; fabricating cutoffs misleads candidates.

---

## 4. Project Objectives
- Build a responsive, accessible web application catering to students and administrators.
- Develop a deterministic, rule-based backend admission predictor that enforces university eligibility prerequisites before evaluating competitiveness.
- Transparently explain the mathematical rationale behind every recommendation (High Chance, Moderate Chance, Low Chance, Insufficient Data, Not Eligible).
- Enable multi-college side-by-side comparison (up to 4 colleges) across cutoffs, fees, NAAC grades, and affiliations.
- Provide administrative tools to curate, verify, and maintain college catalogs, degree courses, and historical cutoffs.
- Generate downloadable PDF reports detailing academic benchmarks and student recommendations.

---

## 5. Scope
- **Geographic Focus:** Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, and Pune (extensible architecture for other regions of Maharashtra).
- **Key Programs:**
  - B.Sc. Information Technology (B.Sc. IT)
  - B.Sc. Computer Science (B.Sc. CS)
  - B.Tech. Computer Engineering (B.Tech CE)
  - B.Tech. Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (B.Tech AI&DS)
  - Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS)
  - Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

---

## 6. Target Users
1. **Students / Candidates:**
   - Explore college directories with filters (city, type, fees, NAAC).
   - Enter academic marks (HSC, SSC, Maths marks, MHT-CET/JEE percentile) to run predictions.
   - Bookmark colleges to personal shortlists.
   - Compare up to 4 colleges side-by-side.
   - Download customized PDF reports.
2. **System Administrators:**
   - Manage college records, course offerings, and verification metadata.
   - Add and audit round-by-round historical cutoffs with duplicate detection.
   - Supervise registered student accounts.

---

## 7. Functional Requirements (FR)
- **FR-01: Authentication & Roles:** Secure student registration, password hashing using bcrypt, JWT authentication with HTTP-only cookies and Bearer tokens, role-based route guards.
- **FR-02: Profile Management:** Academic profile tracking (SSC %, HSC %, Board, Stream, Mathematics marks, Category, Entrance score, Budget).
- **FR-03: Rule-Based Predictor:** Server-side engine evaluating mandatory course criteria, category quotas, and cutoff differentials.
- **FR-04: College Catalog & Search:** Real-time text search, multi-factor filtering (city, course, type, NAAC, fees), sorting, and pagination.
- **FR-05: College Details:** Detailed profile displaying university affiliation, contact portals, offered degree programs, and historical cutoffs.
- **FR-06: College Comparison:** Dynamic matrix comparing up to 4 selected institutions with difference highlighting and removal.
- **FR-07: Favourites:** Persistent database-backed bookmarking of target colleges.
- **FR-08: Prediction History:** Chronological log of student prediction simulations with review and deletion capabilities.
- **FR-09: PDF Report Generation:** Backend streaming of branded counseling reports via PDFKit.
- **FR-10: Administrative CRUD:** Full administrative management of colleges, programs, historical cutoffs, and user accounts.

---

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)
- **NFR-01: Security:** Passwords hashed with salt rounds $\ge 10$; HTTP-only cookies; Helmet security headers; rate limiting on authentication and prediction endpoints.
- **NFR-02: Explainability:** Zero black-box predictions; every outcome discloses the mathematical cutoff difference and data source.
- **NFR-03: Performance:** API response times under 200ms for directory queries and under 300ms for prediction calculations.
- **NFR-04: Responsiveness:** Mobile-first layout supporting screen widths from 360px (mobile) to 1920px (desktop).
- **NFR-05: Reliability:** Graceful error handling for missing cutoffs ("Insufficient Data") without fabricating synthetic numbers.
